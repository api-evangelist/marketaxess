MarketAxess is seeking to bring a feature of fast-twitch equity markets to the relatively sedate $9 trillion universe of corporate bonds: the daily closing auction. The electronic trading company plans to launch a daily “closing print” for corporate bonds – the first standardized end-of-day price in a market long defined by opaque, ad hoc quotes.
